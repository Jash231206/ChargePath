Good questions

Is this using real chargers?

Not yet. Today it runs on a simulated charging network placed along real South Indian highway corridors, so you can explore safely. Real Indian charger networks and live status can plug in through the data adapter.

Does it work with my car?

Popular Indian EVs come pre-loaded with approximate figures. For any other car, add its battery size, consumption, max charging power and connector once and it's saved.

Where are my trips stored?

In your browser, on your device. An account version could sync them everywhere.

How does it pick stops?

It looks at chargers you can safely reach, then balances progress, detour, waiting, speed and price against what you told it matters most.
