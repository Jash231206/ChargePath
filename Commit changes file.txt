This platform is an agentic AI-powered EV charging and route planning application designed to reduce decision load and range risk. By connecting vehicle profiling, corridor routing, AI energy prediction, and charger discovery into a single trip flow, it helps drivers manage real constraints like state of charge and battery capacity. Built on a modular architecture, the system combines a deterministic core for safety-critical decisions with swappable adapters for routing, telematics, and charging. Ultimately, it prioritizes trust and transparency by surfacing risk, explaining arrival states, and gracefully adapting when conditions change.
